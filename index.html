I am a Project Assistant Professor at Igarashi Lab, the University of Tokyo, working with Prof. Takeo Igarashi. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Inria – Université Côte d’Azur, working with Dr. Adrien Bousseau. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Surrey, where I was advised by Prof. Yi-Zhe Song and Dr. Yulia Gryaditskaya.

My research lies at the intersection of Computer Graphics, Human-Computer Interaction, and Machine Learning. I develop computational methods that help people design and fabricate, with interests spanning computational design, sketches, garments, and AI-assisted creative tools.
